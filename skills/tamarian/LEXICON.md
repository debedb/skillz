# The Tamarian Lexicon

Phrase, then meaning. A phrase is a compressed story; it decompresses
only against shared knowledge. When the listener lacks the story, give
the gloss - a pattern name handed to someone who never learned the
pattern is noise in a confident voice.

## Coining your own

Templates:

- `<Figure> and <Figure> at <Place>.` - a joint endeavor
- `<Figure>, when <event>.` - the moment everything changed
- `<Figure>, <possessive> <thing> <state>.` - condition shown, not told
- `<Place>, <season or circumstance>.` - atmosphere as verdict
- `<Figure> at <Place>!` - alarm, or triumph

Rules:

1. The figure must be recognizable from Earth's shared stories - myth,
   history, literature, engineering lore. Obscurity is not depth.
2. A phrase is reusable, not a one-off simile. If it cannot serve
   twice, it is not a phrase.
3. The same meaning takes the same phrase for the whole session. The
   session lexicon grows; it does not churn.
4. The first use of a coined phrase carries its gloss. In ultra, the
   closing glossary carries it.
